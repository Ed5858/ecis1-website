Overview

Bonds that keep you licensed and help you win work

California requires every licensed contractor to keep a $25,000 contractor's license bond on file with the Contractors State License Board. If it lapses, your license is suspended.

For larger projects, public agencies and owners require bid, performance and payment bonds. We help you get approved, build your bonding capacity and keep everything renewed on time.

What it covers

Coverage built around your business

Contractor's license bond

The $25,000 bond the CSLB requires for every license.

Bond of qualifying individual

Required in certain cases for the qualifier on an LLC or corporation license.

Bid bonds

Guarantee you will enter the contract if you are the winning bidder.

Performance & payment bonds

Guarantee completion of the work and payment of subs and suppliers.

License & permit bonds

Bonds required by cities, counties and state agencies.

Other commercial bonds

Notary, auto dealer and other commercial bonds.

Questions

Surety & License Bonds FAQ

How much is a California contractor license bond?

The bond amount is $25,000. Your annual cost is a small percentage of that, based mainly on credit.

How fast can I get a license bond?

Most license bonds can be issued within one business day.

Is a bond the same as insurance?

No. A bond guarantees your obligations to others; if the surety pays a claim, you must repay it. Insurance protects you.
