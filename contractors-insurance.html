CSLB requirements

What the Contractors State License Board requires

  1. Contractor's license bond. A $25,000 bond on file for every license.
  2. Workers' comp certificate or exemption. Required with employees. C-39 roofers must carry it regardless.
  3. Liability insurance for LLCs. Contractors licensed as an LLC must also carry liability coverage.

Questions

Contractors Insurance FAQ

How much does contractors insurance cost in California?

It depends on your trade, payroll, revenue, claims history and the limits your contracts require. We compare multiple carriers to show you several price points for the same coverage.

Do I need workers' comp if I work alone?

A sole owner with no employees can generally file an exemption with the CSLB, except roofers (C-39), who must carry workers' comp. Once you hire, coverage is required.

Can you add a general contractor as additional insured?

Yes. We add additional insured, primary and non-contributory and waiver of subrogation wording when the policy allows, and issue the certificate the same day.

Do you file my workers' comp with the CSLB?

Yes. We handle the CSLB workers' comp filing for our clients so your license record stays current.
