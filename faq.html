Business insurance FAQ

Straight answers to the questions California business owners ask us most.

Questions

All questions

Do I need workers' compensation in California if I only have one employee?

Yes. California requires every employer to carry workers' compensation, even with a single part-time employee. Contractors also need a workers' comp certificate or exemption on file with the CSLB.

How fast can I get a certificate of insurance (COI)?

Same business day. Email or call us with the certificate holder details and we turn it around the same day, usually within hours.

What does an independent insurance broker do?

We aren't tied to one insurance company. We compare multiple carriers and recommend the best mix of coverage and price, at no extra cost to you.

What is lessor's risk insurance?

Insurance for owners of commercial buildings leased to tenants. It covers the building and the owner's liability, and is typically much less expensive than a standard business policy.

Can you insure businesses other agents have turned down?

Often, yes. Through specialty and excess & surplus lines markets we place trucking, bars, high-risk trades and other hard-to-place risks.

Do you only work with businesses in Los Angeles?

Our office is in La Crescenta, but we insure businesses across California. Everything can be done by phone, email and e-signature.

Do I need workers' comp if I only have one employee?

Yes. California requires all employers to carry workers' compensation, even for one part-time employee.

How is workers' comp priced?

Premium is based on your payroll, the classification code for each type of work, and your claims history (experience modification). Getting codes right is one of the biggest ways we save clients money.

Is general liability required in California?

State law does not require it for most businesses, but leases, contracts, licensing boards and clients very often do. LLC contractors must carry liability coverage to be licensed with the CSLB.

How fast can I get a certificate?

Same business day. Send us the certificate holder information and any required wording.

Can you insure drivers with tickets or accidents?

Often, yes. We work with specialty markets that consider drivers and vehicles standard carriers decline.

Do I need commercial auto if my employees use their own cars?

You should consider hired and non-owned auto coverage, which protects your business when employees drive their own vehicles on company business.

What does a BOP not cover?

Workers' compensation, commercial auto and professional liability are separate policies.

Is my business eligible for a BOP?

BOPs are designed for small to mid-size businesses with lower-hazard operations. Higher-risk operations may need a custom package, which we also place.

What is the difference between lessor's risk and a BOP?

A BOP is for a business operating on the premises. Lessor's risk is for an owner leasing the building to others.

Should tenants carry their own insurance?

Yes. Your lease should require tenants to carry general liability and name you as additional insured. We can help with the wording.

Who buys builders risk — the owner or the contractor?

It depends on the contract. Either can, and the policy can protect both. We review your contract to make sure everyone is covered.

Does builders risk cover the contractor’s tools?

Usually not. Tools and equipment are covered by an inland marine (tools and equipment) policy.

How much is a California contractor license bond?

The bond amount is $25,000. Your annual cost is a small percentage of that, based mainly on credit.

How fast can I get a license bond?

Most license bonds can be issued within one business day.

How much umbrella coverage do I need?

It depends on your contracts, assets and exposure. Many small businesses start at $1M–$2M; contracts frequently specify the amount.

What's the difference between umbrella and excess?

Excess follows the terms of the underlying policy; an umbrella can sometimes provide broader coverage. We explain the differences in your proposal.

Do California lawyers have to carry malpractice insurance?

It is not mandatory, but attorneys without it generally must disclose that to clients in writing under California Rule of Professional Conduct 1.4.2.

Is legal malpractice insurance claims-made?

Yes, nearly all lawyers policies are claims-made, so keeping continuous coverage and prior acts dates matters.

How much does contractors insurance cost in California?

It depends on your trade, payroll, revenue, claims history and the limits your contracts require. We compare multiple carriers to show you several price points for the same coverage.

Do I need workers' comp if I work alone?

A sole owner with no employees can generally file an exemption with the CSLB, except roofers (C-39), who must carry workers' comp. Once you hire, coverage is required.

Do I need liquor liability if I only serve beer and wine?

Yes. Any business that sells or serves alcohol has liquor liability exposure, and many landlords require the coverage.

Can you insure a bar that was non-renewed?

Often. We work with specialty and excess & surplus markets that write bars and late-night venues.

Can you insure a new trucking company?

Yes. We have markets for new ventures, though experience and driver records strongly affect pricing.

What do you need for a trucking quote?

Vehicle schedule, driver list with license numbers, commodities hauled, radius, and any prior loss runs.

Does my landlord’s insurance cover my store?

No. The landlord's policy covers the building; you need your own coverage for contents, liability and lost income.

Can I increase inventory coverage for the holidays?

Yes. Many policies include a seasonal increase or we can add one.

Do I need product liability if I do not manufacture?

Yes. Distributors and retailers can be named in product claims. Vendor endorsements from manufacturers help but are not a substitute.

Why do importers need product liability?

Because U.S. plaintiffs often cannot reach foreign manufacturers, importers are frequently treated as the responsible party.

Can you insure older apartment buildings?

Often yes, through specialty carriers. Updates to roofs, electrical, plumbing and heating help with eligibility and price.

Should my tenants have renters insurance?

Requiring renters insurance in your lease reduces disputes and claims against your policy.

Paying too much, or not sure you're covered?

Send us your current policy. We'll review it free and tell you honestly if we can do better.
