Overview

One serious claim can exceed your primary limits

A commercial umbrella sits on top of your general liability, commercial auto and employer's liability policies. When a covered claim exhausts those limits, the umbrella pays the rest, up to its own limit.

It is often the most cost-effective way to meet contract requirements for higher limits, and it protects the business you have built from a single catastrophic claim.

Questions

Commercial Umbrella FAQ

How much umbrella coverage do I need?

It depends on your contracts, assets and exposure. Many small businesses start at $1M–$2M; contracts frequently specify the amount.

What's the difference between umbrella and excess?

Excess follows the terms of the underlying policy; an umbrella can sometimes provide broader coverage. We explain the differences in your proposal.
