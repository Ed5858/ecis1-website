Overview

California homeowners insurance has changed. We know the options.

Many standard carriers have pulled back from California, especially in high-fire-risk areas like the foothills. That doesn't mean you're out of options.

We compare specialty and excess & surplus carriers, high-value home programs, and the California FAIR Plan paired with a difference-in-conditions policy to rebuild the coverage the FAIR Plan leaves out.

What it covers

Coverage designed for your home

Dwelling

Rebuild cost for your home, including extended replacement cost options.

Other structures

Garages, fences, guest houses and pools.

Personal property

Contents, with options for jewelry, art and collectibles.

Loss of use

Living expenses if you cannot live in your home after a covered loss.

Personal liability

Injuries to guests and damage you cause to others.

FAIR Plan fire coverage paired with a difference-in-conditions policy.

Questions

High-Value & Wildfire-Area Homes FAQ

What is a difference-in-conditions (DIC) policy?

A DIC policy pairs with the California FAIR Plan to add the coverage the FAIR Plan leaves out, such as liability, theft and water damage.

Can you help if my homeowners policy was non-renewed?

Yes. Call us as soon as you receive the notice so we have time to shop every option.
