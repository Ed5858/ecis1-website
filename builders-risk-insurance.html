Overview

Your project is most vulnerable before it is finished

Builders risk covers the structure under construction plus materials on site, in transit and in storage, against fire, theft, vandalism, wind and other covered causes of loss.

Lenders usually require it, and many contracts specify who must buy it. We set the policy term to match your schedule, help with extensions if the project runs long, and coordinate with your general liability.

What it covers

Coverage built around your business

Structure under construction

The building itself as it goes up.

Materials & supplies

On site, in transit and at temporary storage locations.

Theft & vandalism

Including theft of building materials from the job site.

Soft costs

Optional coverage for delayed opening and extra interest or fees.

Renovations

Coverage options for remodels of existing buildings.

Three- to twelve-month terms, with extensions available.

Questions

Builders Risk FAQ

Who buys builders risk — the owner or the contractor?

It depends on the contract. Either can, and the policy can protect both. We review your contract to make sure everyone is covered.

Does builders risk cover the contractor’s tools?

Usually not. Tools and equipment are covered by an inland marine (tools and equipment) policy.

Can I insure an ADU build?

Yes. We place builders risk for ADUs and major residential remodels.
