Overview

Your personal auto policy probably excludes your work

Personal auto policies typically exclude vehicles used mainly for business, and they rarely provide the limits your contracts require. A commercial auto policy covers the vehicles your business owns, leases or uses, and the employees who drive them.

Commercial auto has become one of the hardest lines to place in California. Because we have access to several standard carriers plus specialty and wholesale markets, we can usually find coverage even after a non-renewal, a tough loss history or a newer driver.

Questions

Commercial Auto FAQ

Can you insure drivers with tickets or accidents?

Often, yes. We work with specialty markets that consider drivers and vehicles standard carriers decline.

Do I need commercial auto if my employees use their own cars?

You should consider hired and non-owned auto coverage, which protects your business when employees drive their own vehicles on company business.

How is commercial auto priced?

By vehicle type and use, garaging location, radius of operation, driver records and loss history.
