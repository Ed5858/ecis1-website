Buying guide

How to buy commercial insurance in California

1. Start with what's required. California requires workers' compensation for any business with employees, and commercial auto liability for business vehicles. Contractors must meet CSLB bond and workers' comp requirements.

2. Read your contracts and lease. Landlords, general contractors and clients often require general liability with specific limits and endorsements such as additional insured and waiver of subrogation.

3. Protect what you own and your income. Property and business income coverage — often bundled in a business owners policy — keep you going after a fire or other covered loss.

4. Compare apples to apples. Two quotes are only comparable if limits, deductibles, endorsements and exclusions match. Our proposals lay them side by side.

5. Plan for growth. Umbrella limits, bonding capacity and accurate payroll and revenue figures keep you ready for bigger jobs and smoother audits.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need workers' compensation in California if I only have one employee?

Yes. California requires every employer to carry workers' compensation, even with a single part-time employee. Contractors also need a workers' comp certificate or exemption on file with the CSLB.

How fast can I get a certificate of insurance (COI)?

Same business day. Email or call us with the certificate holder details and we turn it around the same day, usually within hours.

What does an independent insurance broker do?

We aren't tied to one insurance company. We compare multiple carriers and recommend the best mix of coverage and price, at no extra cost to you.

What is lessor's risk insurance?

Insurance for owners of commercial buildings leased to tenants. It covers the building and the owner's liability, and is typically much less expensive than a standard business policy.

Can you insure businesses other agents have turned down?

Often, yes. Through specialty and excess & surplus lines markets we place trucking, bars, high-risk trades and other hard-to-place risks.

Do you only work with businesses in Los Angeles?

Our office is in La Crescenta, but we insure businesses across California. Everything can be done by phone, email and e-signature.

Paying too much, or not sure you're covered?

Send us your current policy. We'll review it free and tell you honestly if we can do better.
