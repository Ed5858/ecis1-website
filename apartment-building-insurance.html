Overview

Your rental property is an investment. Insure it like one.

Apartment and rental property policies cover the building, your liability as a landlord and the rent you lose after a covered loss. Habitational risks have become harder to place in California, especially older buildings and those in wildfire areas.

We work with standard, specialty and excess & surplus carriers to place apartments, mixed-use and commercial buildings — and with lessor's risk policies for owners leasing commercial space.

What it covers

Coverage built around your business

Building

Fire, wind, water damage and other covered causes of loss.

Landlord liability

Tenant and visitor injuries in common areas.

Loss of rents

Rental income lost while repairs are made.

Ordinance or law

Code upgrade costs when rebuilding.

Umbrella

Higher liability limits for larger properties.

Lessor's risk

For commercial buildings leased to businesses.

Questions

Apartment & Landlord Insurance FAQ

Can you insure older apartment buildings?

Often yes, through specialty carriers. Updates to roofs, electrical, plumbing and heating help with eligibility and price.

Should my tenants have renters insurance?

Requiring renters insurance in your lease reduces disputes and claims against your policy.
