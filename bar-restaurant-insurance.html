Overview

Busy kitchens and full dining rooms come with real risk

Restaurants combine customer slip-and-falls, kitchen fires, employee injuries and, if you serve alcohol, liquor liability. A standard business policy often leaves gaps.

We build restaurant programs that include liquor liability, food spoilage, equipment breakdown and business income, and we shop specialty markets for bars and late-night venues that standard carriers won't write.

What it covers

Coverage built around your business

Liquor liability

Claims arising from serving alcohol, required for many leases and liquor licenses.

General liability

Customer injuries, including slip-and-falls and foodborne illness claims.

Property & equipment

Kitchen equipment, furniture, inventory and tenant improvements.

Food spoilage

Lost inventory from power outages or refrigeration breakdown.

Business income

Lost income while you rebuild after a covered fire or loss.

Workers' compensation

Cuts, burns and slips are among the most common kitchen injuries.

Questions

Restaurant & Bar Insurance FAQ

Do I need liquor liability if I only serve beer and wine?

Yes. Any business that sells or serves alcohol has liquor liability exposure, and many landlords require the coverage.

Can you insure a bar that was non-renewed?

Often. We work with specialty and excess & surplus markets that write bars and late-night venues.
