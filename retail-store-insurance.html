Overview

Protect your inventory, your customers and your income

Retail stores face slip-and-fall claims, theft, fire and the risk of being closed after a loss. Most retailers are best served by a business owners policy, customized with the right endorsements.

We compare BOPs and package policies from several carriers and make sure your inventory limits keep up with seasonal peaks.

What it covers

Coverage built around your business

Inventory & contents

Stock, fixtures, displays and equipment.

General liability

Customer injuries and property damage.

Theft & crime

Burglary, robbery and employee dishonesty.

Business income

Lost income while you are closed after a covered loss.

Signs & glass

Exterior signs and plate glass.

Product liability

Claims from products you sell.

Questions

Retail Store Insurance FAQ

Does my landlord’s insurance cover my store?

No. The landlord's policy covers the building; you need your own coverage for contents, liability and lost income.

Can I increase inventory coverage for the holidays?

Yes. Many policies include a seasonal increase or we can add one.
