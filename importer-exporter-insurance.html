Overview

Importers are often treated as the manufacturer

When the overseas manufacturer is out of reach of U.S. courts, the importer is often the one sued for a defective product. Importers also carry cargo risk from the factory to your warehouse.

We place product liability with carriers that write imported goods, and coordinate cargo coverage so there are no gaps between ocean, port and inland transit.

Why do importers need product liability?

Because U.S. plaintiffs often cannot reach foreign manufacturers, importers are frequently treated as the responsible party.
