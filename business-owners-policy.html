Overview

More protection for less than buying it piece by piece

A BOP combines general liability with commercial property and business income (loss of income) coverage in a single package. Because carriers bundle them, a BOP usually costs less than buying each coverage separately.

We compare BOPs from multiple carriers and add the endorsements that matter for your business — like equipment breakdown, spoilage, employee dishonesty or hired and non-owned auto.

What it covers

Coverage built around your business

General liability

Third-party injury and property damage claims.

Building & contents

Your building (if owned), inventory, furniture, fixtures and equipment.

Business income

Lost income and ongoing expenses if a covered loss shuts you down.

Equipment breakdown

Mechanical and electrical breakdown of equipment (often optional).

Crime & theft

Options for employee dishonesty and money and securities.

Data breach & cyber

Optional coverage for data breach response costs.

Questions

Business Owners Policy (BOP) FAQ

What does a BOP not cover?

Workers' compensation, commercial auto and professional liability are separate policies.

Is my business eligible for a BOP?

BOPs are designed for small to mid-size businesses with lower-hazard operations. Higher-risk operations may need a custom package, which we also place.

Can I add business income coverage?

Business income is usually included in a BOP; we help set the right amount and waiting period.
